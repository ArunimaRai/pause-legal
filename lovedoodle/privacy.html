Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 24, 2026

Love Doodle ("the App", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect location data
  • We do not collect contacts or address book data
  • We do not track browsing activity
  • We do not sell or share your personal data with advertisers
  • We do not use analytics or tracking SDKs

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Deliver the service: Store and transmit doodles between paired users
  • Push notifications: Notify you when your partner sends a doodle
  • Account management: Authentication, pairing, and subscription status
  • Customer support: Respond to feedback you submit through the App

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • AWS Cognito — authentication (Apple Sign-In, Google Sign-In)
  • Amazon S3 — doodle image storage
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) — push notifications
  • RevenueCat — in-app purchase management
  • Apple App Store / Google Play Store — payment processing

Each service has its own privacy policy governing their handling of your data.

5. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. When you delete your account:

  • Your profile information is permanently deleted
  • Your doodle images are permanently deleted
  • Your pairing data is removed

6. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), secure authentication tokens, and access-controlled cloud infrastructure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn we have collected such information, we will delete it promptly.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Delete your account and all associated data (available in-app)
  • Withdraw consent by deleting your account

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the App. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.

10. Contact
