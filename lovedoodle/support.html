Frequently Asked Questions

How do I pair with my partner?

Open the app and tap the Pair tab. You'll get a 6-character invite code — share it with your partner. Once they enter the code, you're connected and can start sending doodles to each other.

How does the home screen widget work?

Long-press your home screen, tap the + button, and search for "Love Doodle". Add the widget and it will automatically display the latest doodle from your partner. It updates every time a new doodle is sent.

My widget isn't updating. What should I do?

Make sure notifications are enabled for Love Doodle in your iPhone Settings. The widget updates when a new doodle arrives via push notification. Try removing and re-adding the widget if it's stuck.

How does the free trial work?

New users get a 7-day free trial with full access to all features. After the trial, you can unlock lifetime access with a single one-time purchase — no subscriptions, no recurring charges.

Does my partner also need to pay?

No. When one partner purchases Love Doodle, both partners get full access. One purchase covers both of you.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open Love Doodle on your new device, sign in with the same account, then go to the menu and tap "Restore purchase". Your lifetime access will be restored automatically.

What is the Love Tree?

The Love Tree is a shared plant that grows when you and your partner send doodles regularly. Keep a daily streak to watch it bloom. If you go a few days without doodling, it starts to wilt — a gentle reminder to check in on each other.

Can I unpair from my partner?

Yes. Open the menu and tap "Unpair". This will disconnect you from your partner and clear your shared doodle history. You can pair with someone new afterward.

How do I delete my account?

Open the menu and tap "Delete account". This will permanently remove your account, all your doodles, and your pairing data. This action cannot be undone.
